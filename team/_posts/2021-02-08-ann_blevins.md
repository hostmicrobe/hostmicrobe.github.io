Ann completed her Ph.D. and postdoc in the laboratory of Danielle Bassett at UPenn, where she used network theory to address questions in cognitive neuroscience.  She joined the lab 2021 and took the reigns on the backend development of our [microbiomeDB](http://microbiomedb.org/) project.  Her amazing work resulted in the data analysis and visualization tools available on the website today.
